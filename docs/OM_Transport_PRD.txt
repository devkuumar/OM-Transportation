## 2. Problem

The business currently relies heavily on offline visibility, phone communication, physical signage and word-of-mouth. Potential customers have no strong digital destination where they can:
- Understand services
- See vehicle options
- Submit transport requirements
- Contact the business quickly
- Find the office
- Understand the commission-agent service
- Join the business as a transport partner

## 3. Product Goal

Create a professional, fast, mobile-first website that turns visitors into:
1. Transport enquiries
2. WhatsApp conversations
3. Phone calls
4. Quote requests
5. Vehicle-partner leads

## 4. Target Users

### Customer A: Individual / Small Business
Needs a truck for moving goods.

### Customer B: SME / B2B Buyer
Needs recurring full-load or part-load transportation.

### Customer C: Industrial Customer
Needs commercial/special vehicle arrangements.

### Customer D: Vehicle Owner
Owns a truck and wants transportation opportunities.

### Customer E: Local Search User
Finds OM Transport through Google/local search and needs contact/location information.

## 5. Core User Journeys

### Journey 1: Request a Truck
Home → Vehicle Options → Select Vehicle → Transport Form → WhatsApp/Call

### Journey 2: General Enquiry
Home → WhatsApp → Pre-filled enquiry

### Journey 3: Find Office
Contact → Map → Get Directions

### Journey 4: Partner
Partner With Us → Partner Form → Submission

### Journey 5: Business Customer
Home → Services → Get Quote → Quote Form → Contact

## 6. Functional Requirements

### FR-01 Homepage
The system shall display:
- Company name
- Hero CTA
- Services
- Vehicle options
- Service coverage
- Contact options

### FR-02 Vehicle Options
The system shall display:
- 14 FT
- 17 FT
- 19 FT
- 20 FT
- 22 FT
- Tata Ace

The system shall not invent payload or technical specifications.

### FR-03 Transport Quote
The system shall provide a quote/request form with:
- Customer name
- Company
- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Pickup
- Delivery
- Date
- Vehicle
- Load type
- Cargo type
- Weight
- Dimensions
- Notes

### FR-04 Vehicle-Specific Request
Selecting a vehicle shall pre-fill the vehicle requirement in the form.

### FR-05 WhatsApp
The website shall create a pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry.

### FR-06 Phone
Phone numbers shall be clickable.

### FR-07 Maps
The website shall provide the client Google Maps location.

### FR-08 Services
Display:
- Full Load
- Part Load
- Special Vehicle Arrangement
- Commission Agent
- Local Transportation
- Long-Distance Transportation
- All India Service
- Business/Commercial Transport

### FR-09 Partner Registration
Provide a form for commercial vehicle owners.

### FR-10 Gallery
Use client-provided office/business photographs.

### FR-11 Business Information
Display verified legal/trade/GST/location information.

### FR-12 Responsive Design
Support mobile, tablet and desktop.
